    What the numbers don't say

    Most of the signal is where cars start. The grid-only guess already gets 83.7% in 2024, so the model's 84.1% is a small step. 2024 is also the year the two are closest; in 2022 and 2023 the model was 4.5 and 5.5 points ahead.

    An earlier version of the team-form inputs let one driver's row see his teammate's result from the same race. That is fixed here: team form is averaged per race, then lagged. The scores barely moved.

    Weather, tyre strategy, penalties after qualifying and breakdowns are not modelled.

    Data: Jolpica F1 API (Ergast-compatible), seasons 2015–2024. Model: scikit-learn Random Forest, 300 trees, depth 6. Every season's model is trained only on earlier seasons.
